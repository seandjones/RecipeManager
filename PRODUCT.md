# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Family members planning meals for a week or month who need a quick way to capture recipes, organize ingredient lists, and coordinate shopping.

## Product Purpose
RecipeManager helps households turn meal ideas into actionable weekly or monthly plans by connecting recipes with ingredient lists and real-time list collaboration.

## Brand Personality
Approachable, refined, practical.
The tone should feel friendly and clear, with calm confidence instead of technical jargon.

## Anti-references
Avoid generic, plain, auto-generated grid-heavy interfaces with repetitive card layouts and formulaic visual treatment.

## Design Principles
1. Plan-to-action continuity: moving from recipe to ingredient list should feel like one connected flow.
2. Friendly efficiency: interactions should be simple and warm while minimizing steps.
3. Shared clarity: collaboration states and permissions should always be obvious.
4. Everyday trust: content and controls should prioritize readability and predictable behavior.
5. Calm focus: visual emphasis should guide decisions without noisy decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG AA contrast and interaction standards, and support reduced-motion preferences for users sensitive to animation.
